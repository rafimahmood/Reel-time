How to dance

Follow the figure

Walk the travelling figures yourself at the dance's pace. In a circle hold LEFT for eight steps to the left, then RIGHT for eight back. Turns carry you while you hold TURN. Stay inside the ring, which is where the dance says you should be right now. The dotted trail shows the next two bars. From above, WASD or arrows move you around the floor.

First person

You always face the way the dance goes. Push forward to follow the gold arrows on the floor to the see-through you. Drag or use ←/→ to glance round; it springs back. V switches views mid-dance.

Turn and release

Hold TURN (T) when the bar at the top reaches green and you dance round together. The dance gives the right hand for you.

Let go on time

Release as the turn ends. Let go early and your partner spins off past their place; hang on and you drag them out of it. Get it right and you both land on your spots.

Setting

Press SET (Space) when the marker in the bar at the top reaches the green. You step right, left, right and clap on your own. Once you have learned more steps, the bar names the one it wants: Space pas de basque, F high cut, G Highland fling, J heel and toe. The right key scores more for the fancier steps; the wrong key costs you points.

Flair

H hooch at the end of an 8-bar phrase. R twirl while travelling. F high cut on a setting beat. B helicopter: spin fast with your arms out, mid-phrase, instead of a set, or in the middle of the Eightsome Reel. P pauses.

Flair pulse and new moves

Learn moves at the outfitter. The FLAIR bar fills over two bars: press in its green at the end for C hands-up clap, X spring (land in the ring for a bonus) or K pirouette (stay in the ring all bar). Z heel click scores on any half-bar beat while you are on the figure. N bow or curtsey in the last bar of a figure, once each. Different moves in a row build a chain that multiplies the score; a miss breaks it. Each move costs energy, and training raises the energy bar, speed of refill, window and points.
